MultiReader

A unique document reading experience, text extraction from images/PDF, and professional text-to-speech tailored to your needs.

Key Features

  • Document Support: Smooth reading for formats like Word (DOCX), TXT, and HTML.
  • Smart OCR: High accuracy text extraction from PDFs and images locally (Offline) or via Gemini API.
  • Secure Library: Exceptional saving system; files remain safe even if app data is cleared.
  • Advanced TTS: Full control over reading speed, pitch, and smart paragraph navigation.
  • Accessibility: Full and perfect support for screen readers (TalkBack).
